# Problem Statement & SDG Alignment

## Problem statement

How might we use AI to guide everyday waste decisions so that
households and communities can segregate waste correctly at the
source?

Most households want to segregate waste correctly but don't know
which category a given item belongs to — is a used pizza box
recyclable? Is a milk carton wet or dry waste? Rules also vary by
city and municipality, adding to the confusion.

## SDG alignment

**Primary — SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities.** Better
municipal solid-waste management is a core target of SDG 11: cutting
the environmental footprint of cities by improving how waste is
handled at the source.

**Secondary — SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production.**
Correct segregation increases the share of waste that is actually
recycled or composted, reducing landfill load and supporting circular
use of materials.

## Target users

- **Households** — everyday residents unsure how to sort daily waste
- **Municipal workers** — field staff who need quick, consistent
  guidance to share with residents
- **Housing societies & RWAs** — communities setting up shared
  segregation and composting systems
- **Schools & colleges** — campuses building sustainability habits

## Expected impact

- Higher segregation accuracy → less contamination of recyclable
  streams
- Less landfill burden → more waste diverted to composting/recycling
- Consistent guidance at scale → every resident gets the same
  accurate answer, instantly
